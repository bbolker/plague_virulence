## my thoughts about themes

* Explaining spatial/geographic and temporal patterns (appearance/disappearance, spread patterns ...) (Metapopulation? Sylvatic cycle/reservoir hosts? Source/sink dynamics?)
* How to get more information about parameters/processes? Can we do phylodynamics? Are existing phylodynamic/phylogeographic tools suitable for plague?
* More generally, how to combine information (history/archaeology, modern-day phenotypic studies, genetics/genomics, math modeling)?

## questions

Addresses historical pandemics, macro-level dynamics, and the mathematical/computational frameworks needed to simulate them.

- **Christian**: Why does the plague disappear and then return?
- **Ann**: What best accounts for the apparent disappearance of Y. pestis from Europe, 1650-1750CE
- **Christian**: Why did it not return to Europe during the third pandemic?
- **Sebastian**: Can we tease out transmission patterns from genetic data (modelling unsampled populations/phylodynamics)?
- **Guillem**: Are there consequences of host-adaptation processes for plague epidemiology?
- **Dan**: Does the Ebola latency phylogeny mirror Y. pestis over time?
- **Maria**: Which modelling frameworks can realistically integrate mixed-route variability and parameter uncertainties (e.g., ghost demes for unsampled vectors)?
- **Nukhet**: How did changes in Y. pestis, including virulence, shape the course of the Second Plague Pandemic?
- **Nukhet**: How does Y. pestis interact with host ecology, environment, and social conditions to shape plague dynamics?
- **David/Jonathan/Ben**: is the enhanced possibility of colonization (through increased survival while infected), as mentioned in Sidhu et al. (2025), sufficient to give pla-reduced strains an evolutionary advantage when patches are small and far apart?
- **David/Jonathan/Ben**: how do the changes in pla copy number affect rat-to-flea transmission probabilities? (not necessarily an experiment anyone wants to do ...)]
- **Tim:** Did virulence attenuation mean plague killed fewer of the rodents it infected but not fewer of the people?
- **Tim:** Did plague really need steam ships to globalize (were late-stage sail ships not enough)?
- **Tim:** Why did ~10% of Florence die in the plague outbreak of 1630 and ~50% of Milan?
- **Tim:** Why was third pandemic plague mortality so heavily concentrated in one region of the world?
- **Tim:** Why did the second pandemic, in the Euro-Mediterranean area end?
- **Tim:** Where can we concretely identify first pandemic plague before 541 and second pandemic plague before 1330?
- **Tim:** Why do we think the first pandemic ended c.750?
- **Tim:** What regions and periods have historians of plague yet to focus on?
